## photo:g_desk
[title] I typed one prompt. Everything you are watching came out of it.

## pipeline
Here is the whole journey, step by step, from one sentence to a finished video.

## prompt
It starts with a prompt, in plain words. Make a short video about my workflow, and keep it simple.

## script
Claude writes the script. Every scene, every line, and where each picture goes.

## images
Then the pictures. Claude picks a few search words for each scene, and finds a photo that fits.

## apis
APIs do the fetching. An API is simply how one program asks another for something.

## sites
A free photo site sends the pictures. A voice site reads the script in my own cloned voice.

## effects
Then the effects: a **whoosh** on every cut, words that pop, and music under the voice.

## renderer
The renderer turns all of it into video, drawing one frame at a time, thirty every second.

## video
And out comes the video, with captions on every word, checked scene by scene before it ships.
